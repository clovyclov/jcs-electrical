Common Questions

EV Charger Installation FAQ

How much does it cost to install a home EV charger? +
Installation costs typically range depending on the distance from your main electrical panel to your desired charger location, whether a panel upgrade or subpanel is needed, and municipal permit requirements. We provide an exact, upfront flat-rate quote before any work starts so you never face hidden fees.
Do I need a 200 Amp panel upgrade for an EV charger? +
Not always! During our inspection, we perform a load calculation on your main electrical panel. If your panel has adequate available capacity (usually 50 amps of spare load), we can install a dedicated 240V circuit immediately. If your panel is full or 100A, we can discuss panel upgrade options or load management devices.
Can you install Tesla Wall Connectors and non-Tesla chargers? +
Yes! We install all major EV charging hardware, including Tesla Wall Connectors, Tesla Universal Connectors (for J1772 & NACS), ChargePoint Home Flex, Grizzl-E, JuiceBox, and heavy-duty 240V NEMA 14-50 outlets for portable chargers.
Do you handle municipal electrical permits and inspections? +
Yes, 100%. As licensed, bonded, and insured WA electrical contractors (License # JCSECEL760DM), we pull all necessary city/county electrical permits and schedule inspector sign-offs so your installation is completely safe and code-compliant.
