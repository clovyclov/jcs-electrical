Privacy Policy

Last Updated: February 2026

At JCS Electrical ("we," "us," or "our"), keeping your personal information safe and respected is our priority. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, protect, and handle your information when you visit our website go.jcselectricalseattle.com or interact with our electrical services.

When you request a quote, fill out a contact form, or call our team, we may collect personal details including:

  • Full Name

  • Service Address & Zip Code
  • Details about your requested electrical project

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect strictly to fulfill your requests and provide quality electrical services, including:

  • Contacting you to schedule estimates and confirm service appointments.

  • Answering customer service inquiries and technical questions.

3. Text Messaging & SMS Consent (A2P 10DLC Standards)

By providing your phone number to JCS Electrical through our quote request form, phone calls, or digital inquiries, you consent to receive transactional and operational text messages (SMS) regarding your estimate, appointment scheduling, and project status updates.

Strict Non-Sharing Policy: No mobile information will be shared with third parties/affiliates for marketing/promotional purposes. All the above categories exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties.

Opt-Out & Support: You can cancel or opt out of SMS communications at any time by replying STOP to any message you receive from us. For assistance, reply HELP or contact our team directly at (425) 696-2234. Message frequency varies based on project interaction. Message and data rates may apply.

4. Data Protection & Security

We implement robust technical and organizational security measures to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. We do not sell, rent, trade, or lease your personal information to third parties.
