Panel Upgrade FAQ

How do I know if I need a panel upgrade? +
Common signs: breakers that trip repeatedly, flickering or dimming lights, a buzzing or burning smell near the panel, an old fuse box, or not enough capacity to add an EV charger, generator, or new circuits. If any of that sounds familiar, it's worth having us take a look.
How long does a panel upgrade take? +
Most residential panel upgrades take a single day of on-site work, though the full timeline can depend on permitting and utility coordination for the service disconnect/reconnect. We'll walk you through the expected timeline before work starts.
Do you handle permits and utility coordination? +
Yes, 100%. Panel upgrades typically require a city/county electrical permit and coordination with your utility company to safely disconnect and reconnect power. We handle all of that so you don't have to.
Are you licensed and insured? +
Yes. JCS Electrical is a licensed, bonded & insured WA electrical contractor (License # JCSECEL760DM). We pull permits and leave every panel upgrade inspection-ready.
