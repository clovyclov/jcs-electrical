Questions

Frequently asked

Do you offer free consultations? +
We offer complimentary phone consultations to help you plan with confidence. For an in-person visit, we charge $89, which covers an energy audit, electrical inspection, needs discussion, and expert recommendations — a no-obligation way to get informed before you proceed.
How fast can an electrician come out? +
We offer same-day service for most routine jobs and 24/7 immediate dispatch for emergency repairs.
Are your electricians licensed and insured? +
Absolutely. Every job is handled by a fully licensed, bonded, and insured electrician (License #EC-004821).
What areas do you serve? +
We proudly serve Pierce, King and Snohomish Counties, including White Center, Tukwila, Burien, SeaTac, Fairwood, Renton, Kent, Federal Way, Auburn, Edgewood, Fife, Tacoma, Sumner, Puyallup, Des Moines, South Center, Dash Point, Shoreline, Woodinville, Edmonds, Cottage Lake, Bellevue, Kirkland, Bothell, Seattle, Redmond, Mercer Island, Marysville, Snohomish, and Everett. Call us to confirm coverage.
Can you install an EV charger at my home? +
Yes, we specialize in Level 2 home EV charger installations for Tesla, ChargePoint, JuiceBox, and all EV models.
What should I do during an electrical emergency? +
If you notice burning smells, sparks, or sudden power loss, shut off your main breaker if safe to do so, and call our 24/7 line at (425) 696-2234 immediately.
